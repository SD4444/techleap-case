Proposed Techleap capability

Make investor requirements visible before startups begin fundraising.

Techleap can collect recurring investor requirements, structure them by company type and financing need, and turn them into practical guidance for startups.

This would help startups build the right evidence earlier and give investors better-prepared financing cases.

Investor requirementsStructured guidanceBetter-prepared startupsFaster investment decisions

The information gap

Startups often learn what investors require after fundraising has begun.

01 · Requirements remain tacit

Investor expectations are distributed across individual funds, partners and financing processes. Startups rarely have a clear view of the evidence required for the next investment decision.

02 · Advice is too generic

A pre-commercial hardware company, biotech business and software company face different technical, commercial and financing requirements. General fundraising advice does not capture these differences.

03 · Feedback is lost

Questions raised during diligence and reasons for declining investment are rarely captured systematically. Other startups therefore encounter the same problems again.

The problem is not a lack of information. It is that the information remains fragmented, inconsistent and difficult to apply.

How it works

Turn repeated investor questions into shared infrastructure.

01 · Collect

Conduct structured interviews with investors, production financiers and specialist lenders. Capture requirements from live diligence processes and declined investments.

02 · Structure

Classify each requirement by sector, company maturity, capital need, use of funds, business model and financing type.

03 · Apply

Translate the requirements into sector guides, evidence checklists and standard financing-case templates that startups can use before approaching investors.

04 · Learn

Track which companies enter diligence, what additional evidence investors request and why investment decisions are delayed or declined. Use these outcomes to update the guidance.

What investors are likely to assess

  • Evidence of buyer demand and willingness to pay
  • A crop-specific validation plan with measurable performance requirements
  • Machine cost, unit economics and expected margins
  • Manufacturing partners, component lead times and working-capital requirements
  • Delivery, maintenance and service capacity
  • A clear separation between verified evidence, assumptions and unresolved risks

Final requirements would be based on structured investor research, not assumed by Techleap.

Delivery

One evidence base, delivered through several formats.

01

Public requirements library

Searchable guidance organised by sector, maturity and financing need.

02

Capital-readiness diagnostic

A structured assessment that produces a tailored evidence checklist for each startup.

03

Financing-case workspace

A private format for organising verified evidence, assumptions, unresolved risks and supporting documents.

04

Investor decision sessions

Small, curated meetings around live financing cases, followed by structured feedback on the remaining investment conditions.

Techleap does not select investments or promise capital. It makes investor requirements easier to understand and financing cases easier to assess.

How to start

Validate the information system before building the platform.

01 · Test two sectors

Begin with agricultural technology and climate hardware. Interview relevant investors, financiers and startups in each sector.

02 · Apply it to live cases

Use the initial framework with a small number of startups preparing real financing rounds. Record where the guidance is useful, incomplete or wrong.

03 · Publish and expand

Release the first sector guides and diagnostic. Add new sectors only when enough investor evidence and live cases exist to support useful guidance.

The benefit

Startups know what to prove. Investors receive stronger cases. Techleap sees where the market repeatedly breaks down.

The same information that helps an individual startup prepare for investment can also reveal recurring financing barriers across sectors. Techleap can use that evidence to improve programmes, convene the right capital providers and identify where broader market intervention is needed.
